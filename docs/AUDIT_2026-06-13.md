# Studio audit — 2026-06-13 (input to GTM Phase 3 cleanup)

Two audits of the v1 surface (V1 flag on, default build): a **static wiring
trace** (every visible control traced onclick → command → implementation;
all P0/P1 claims code-verified) and a **live UX review** (Playwright-driven
first-time-user pass at 1440×900 / 1280×720, kernel live, real AI turns).
A third dynamic crawl was stopped early; coverage below is from the two
completed audits. Actionable checklist lives in GTM_EXECUTION.md Phase 3.

**Theme:** the product works — the AI really searched the library and placed
a 608 bearing — but the first-run experience hides every win. The studio
boots with the camera inside a seeded 40 mm box, so AI placements, compiles,
and library drops produce zero visible change. Nothing is more important to
fix than "you can't see anything happen."
